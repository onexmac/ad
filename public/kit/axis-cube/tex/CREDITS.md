# Axis Cube textures: credits

The surface textures (`*_diff`, `*_nor`, `*_rough`) are CC0 scans from Poly Haven.

The printed matter in the room is real, taken from open collections. Each line gives the work, its maker, the page it came from and the rights statement of that source. Images were cropped to their cells, toned to paper and given a trace of edge and ring wear; nothing was drawn over them.
